StickyNotes — Privacy Policy
最后更新：2026 年 10 月 23 日 · Last updated: October 23, 2026
核 心 声 明

一笔勾销完全在您的设备本地运行。
我们不收集、不存储、不上传、不共享您的任何个人数据。

CORE COMMITMENT

StickyNotes runs entirely on your device.
We do not collect, store, upload, or share any of your personal data.

一、我们收集哪些信息

我们不收集任何信息。

  • 待办内容：您创建的所有待办、已办事项仅保存在您的设备本地，我们无法访问、无法查看。
  • 设备信息：我们不收集设备型号、系统版本、设备标识符等任何信息。
  • 使用数据：我们不记录任何使用行为，不含任何统计分析组件。
  • 位置与权限：我们不请求、不访问您的位置、相册、通讯录等任何敏感权限。

二、数据存储在哪里

您的全部数据保存在本设备的 App 私有存储容器中。

为实现桌面小组件功能，App 与小组件扩展通过 iOS/macOS 系统的 App Groups 机制在同一设备内部共享数据。该共享仅发生在您的设备内部，不经过任何网络传输。

当您删除本 App 时，全部数据将随之永久删除，不可恢复。

三、第三方服务

本 App 当前版本不含任何第三方服务，无广告 SDK、无统计 SDK、无崩溃收集组件。

后续版本如引入 App 内购买（In-App Purchase），购买流程将由 Apple 全权处理，我们不接触、不存储您的任何支付信息。

四、儿童隐私

本 App 适合所有年龄段的用户使用。由于我们不收集任何数据，我们不会有意收集儿童的任何信息。

五、您的权利

您可以随时在 App 内编辑或删除任意待办事项；删除本 App 即可清除全部数据。

您无需向我们提出任何数据请求——因为我们从不持有您的数据。

六、隐私政策的更新

如本政策发生变更，我们将更新本页面并同步修改"最后更新"日期；重大变更将通过 App 版本更新说明告知。

支持与反馈 · Support

常见问题

  • 如何清空全部数据？删除本 App 即可，数据仅存于本机，随之永久删除。
  • 换新手机后数据会同步吗？当前版本为纯本地应用，数据不跨设备同步；换机前请手动处理重要事项。云同步功能已在规划中。
  • 为什么 App 不需要登录？因为没有账号系统——这是我们刻意为之的设计，不是遗漏。

1. Information We Collect

We collect no information.

  • Your content: All to-dos and completed items you create stay solely on your device. We cannot access or view them.
  • Device information: We do not collect device model, OS version, identifiers, or any other device data.
  • Usage data: We record no usage behavior and include no analytics components.
  • Permissions: We never request access to your location, photos, contacts, or any other sensitive data.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

All your data is stored in the app's private container on your device.

To provide the home-screen widget, the app and its widget extension share data through the system App Groups mechanism within the same device only. This sharing never travels over any network.

When you delete the app, all data is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

3. Third-Party Services

The current version of this app includes no third-party services — no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting components.

If a future version introduces In-App Purchases, all purchase processing will be handled entirely by Apple. We never touch or store your payment information.

4. Children's Privacy

This app is suitable for users of all ages. Since we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

5. Your Rights

You may edit or delete any item in the app at any time; deleting the app removes all data entirely.

You never need to send us a data request — because we never hold your data.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "last updated" date. Material changes will be announced in the app's release notes.

Support & Feedback

FAQ

  • How do I erase all data? Delete the app. Data lives only on this device and is permanently removed with it.
  • Will my data sync to a new phone? The current version is fully local; data does not sync across devices. Cloud sync is on the roadmap.
  • Why is there no login? Because there are no accounts — by design, not by oversight.
